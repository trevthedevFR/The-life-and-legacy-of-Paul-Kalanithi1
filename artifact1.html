The CT scan revealing Kalanithi's stage IV lung cancer marks the beginning of his confrontation with mortality. Until this moment, he had been a successful neurosurgeon focused on saving lives. This diagnosis forced him to consider his own death and changed his perspective on life forever. The artifact represents the moment mortality became a reality.

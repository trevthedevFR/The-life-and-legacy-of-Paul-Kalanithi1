Artifact 4: Lucy and Cady

https://med.stanford.edu/news/all-news/2015/03/stanford-neurosurgeon-writer-paul-kalanithi-dies-at-37/_jcr_content/cq:featuredimage.coreimg.jpg/1752011191102/kalanithi-paul.jpg

Kalanithi and his wife Lucy chose to have a child despite his terminal diagnosis. Their daughter, Cady, symbolizes hope, love, and continuity. This artifact shows that accepting death does not mean giving up on life. Instead, Kalanithi continued investing in relationships that gave life meaning.

Curator Reflection

The most important lesson readers can learn from "When Breath Becomes Air" is that life gains meaning because it is limited. Kalanithi's cancer diagnosis forced him to confront mortality, yet he chose to spend his remaining time practicing medicine when possible, raising a family, and sharing his story. His decision to have a daughter despite knowing his life would be short demonstrates courage and hope. Today, this lesson remains relevant because everyone faces uncertainty. Kalanithi reminds readers that accepting mortality can help people focus on what truly matters and live with greater purpose.

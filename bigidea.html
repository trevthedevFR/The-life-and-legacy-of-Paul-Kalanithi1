The Big Idea

Paul Kalanithi develops the theme of mortality by transforming from a doctor who cared for dying patients into a patient facing his own death. The CT Scan Diagnosis introduces the reality of mortality. The White Coat shows his struggle to redefine himself as his illness progresses. "The fact of death is unsettling. Yet there is no other way to live" demonstrates his growing acceptance of death. Through Lucy and Cady, he shows that life remains meaningful despite uncertainty. By writing When Breath Becomes Air, Kalanithi leaves behind a legacy that encourages others to find purpose even when confronted by mortality.

Curator Introduction

In When Breath Becomes Air, Paul Kalanithi explores what it means to face mortality and accept death. After spending years as a neurosurgeon treating critically ill patients, he received a diagnosis of terminal lung cancer. The memoir follows his journey as he learns that death is not simply something that happens to others but a reality everyone must face. Visitors to this exhibit will see how Kalanithi's experiences reveal that accepting mortality can help people live more meaningful lives.
